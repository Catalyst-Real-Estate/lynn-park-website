About the Park

Lynn Corporate Park is a four-building light industrial campus on 10.6 acres in the Wixom/Novi area of southeast Michigan. Built between 2003 and 2014, all buildings feature steel-frame construction with decorative CMU exteriors, 19'–23' clear heights, and modern building systems.

The park is home to over 30 businesses including distributors, manufacturers, tech companies, service contractors, and specialty retailers. Our onsite management team provides responsive maintenance, flexible lease terms, and a property that's always well-kept.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of businesses are in the park?

Our tenants include distributors, light manufacturers, service contractors (plumbing, electrical, pest control), tech companies, specialty retailers, and professional offices. The park is zoned M-1 Light Industrial, which accommodates a wide range of uses.

What are the warehouse clear heights?

Warehouse clear heights range from 19' to 23' depending on the building and suite. Building A has 18' heights, while Buildings B, C, and D offer 20' heights.

What condition are the spaces in?

All spaces are professionally maintained and move-in ready. Our onsite management team keeps the buildings in excellent condition, and we're happy to walk you through any suite so you can see the space firsthand.

Who do I contact for utilities?

Electric: DTE Energy (800) 477-4747. Gas: Consumers Energy (800) 477-5050. Water: City of Wixom Water Dept (248) 960-0870. Our office can help you get set up.

Is there onsite management?

Yes. Our management office is in Suite A-206, Building A. We provide onsite maintenance, a secure mail room, and responsive property management. Call us at (248) 348-1923.

Do I need to register my business?

Yes, all tenants must register with the City of Wixom. Our office can assist you with the process.

How much does warehouse space cost in the Wixom / Novi area?

Rates at Lynn Corporate Park start at $0.80/SF + NNN for warehouse and flex space. Final pricing depends on suite size, term length, and tenant improvements. Contact our office for a current quote on available suites.

Can I use the space for my contracting business (plumbing, electrical, HVAC)?

Absolutely. The park is zoned M-1 Light Industrial, which permits contractor offices, parts storage, vehicle staging, and related uses. Many of our tenants are trade contractors who value the grade-level door access, proximity to supply houses, and I-96 freeway access.
